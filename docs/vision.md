# Ond — vision

*Ond* (Old Norse *ǫnd*): breath. Working name.

## What it is

A drone organ and harmonium you can play, with a wave of sound that fills a room, and enough
variation to use it actively. Electronic wear on top: tape, skipping, a bad cable.

References: Bon Iver *22, A Million*, James Blake, Ólafur Arnalds, and above all Nils Frahm.
What they share: the instrument is only half the sound; the other half is what happens to it on
the way (mechanics, air, tape, room, digital breakage).

## Starting point

Clipper on the FM-1, as heard in the emulator: the WHEEL organ presets (CHAPEL, REED ORGAN,
SOFT FLUTE, STRING ORG), the drone presets, DUST, the reverbs (HALL, SHIMMER) and the chorus.
Morten: "This is exactly what I want." Everything is built from there.

## Our twist, in order

1. **Bellows** — the breath of a harmonium: slow swells in level, tone and a tiny pitch sag,
   from calm breathing to uneven pumping. Played from a knob or the mod wheel.
2. **Drone layer** — root and fifth standing under everything, from the existing drone engines.
3. **Tape and skipping** — DUB-1's tape echo (quarter-rate tape, darker each repeat, wow, tape
   stop, stutter, freeze) and a bad-cable stage (crackle, dropouts, hum that comes and goes).
4. **Harmonising** — chord mode (one key, a chord) and prism mode (one note spreads to a voicing
   that drifts a little each time).
5. **Reed voice** — a real free-reed (harmonium) tone next to the drawbar organ, built from
   measurements of a real harmonium.
6. **One WEAR macro** — clean to broken on one knob.

## Where it runs

- FM-1 firmware (the fork itself).
- Desktop / Ableton: the same DSP as a plugin (or the fm1-emu plugin running our firmware).
- Phone: the same DSP in the browser.
