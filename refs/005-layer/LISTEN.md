# 005 — the layer: a second sound under or beside the first (Clipper's LAYER)

Status: **ready to listen**. Nothing new in the DSP: Clipper 0.3's LAYER (voice.c `layer_*`, the FM-1's EDIT >
LAYER page), now on Ond's screen (the LAYER button). Off by default: with it off, the baseline's samples.

## The files (44.1 kHz, 12 s, C-E-G held 0.1–9 s, through the plugin)

- `before_chapel.wav`: CHAPEL alone.
- `after_chapel_drone-layer.wav`: CHAPEL with HYBRID DRONE layered under every note, an octave down
  (MODE LAYER, OCT -1).
- `after_chapel_drone-layer-dark.wav`: the same, the layer's FILT at -30 (its low-pass: darker, further back).
- `after_chapel_drone-split-e4.wav`: SPLIT at E4: C4 plays the drone, E4 and G4 the organ (one hand each).

## What to listen for

1. Does the drone sit under the organ as the vision's "drone layer" (005) wants, or does it want something the
   layer cannot do yet (root and fifth only, its own slow swell)?
2. The layer's level (LEVEL, -4 dB as the main sound's) against the organ.
