# 008c stage 2 (plumbing) — Brudd's hits: your recordings on the pads

Status: **ready to try**. The sampler only; the sounds are yours (docs/rhythm-breaks.md: no hit is made by
Claude). Put WAV files in `Documents\Ond\Hits` (yours: `OneDrive\Dokumenter\Ond\Hits`, made on Ond's first
start): the first eight by name are hits 1–8 (name them `1 thump.wav`, `2 felt.wav`... to order them). RELOAD in
the BRUDD view after changing the folder.

- Played from: the BRUDD view's third row (click), the MPK's bank A on channel 10 (notes 36–43, until learned),
  or LEARN (click a hit, hit the pad). Velocity sets the level (squared, as a key's).
- Into the mix before DUST and the breakers: DUST dirties them, a STOP or STUTTER takes them along.
- Any WAV: 16 / 24 / 32-bit or float, mono or stereo, any rate (brought to 44.1 kHz with the plugin's resampler),
  up to 20 s.

## The files (test samples from ond-samples, CC0, not a choice of sounds)

- `before_chapel.wav`: CHAPEL alone.
- `after_hits_claves-clap-bass.wav`: VCSL claves at 2.0 s, VCSL clap (a 48 kHz file) at 3.0 s, Felucca's E1
  bass (22 kHz) at 4.0 s, claves at 5.0 and again at 5.25 s.
- `after_hits_48k-session.wav`: the clap with Ond at 48 kHz.
